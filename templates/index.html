UDM Portal

Unified Data Management — the canonical digital record for every railway component. Track dates, warranties, lifecycle status, and inspection history.

  • Component date tracking (supply, warranty, inspection)
  • Warranty status & expiry alerts
  • Lifecycle state machine (REGISTERED → DELIVERED → INSTALLED)
  • Vendor performance analytics
  • Real-time sync from GaugeMarket

TMS Portal

Transport & Monitoring System — owns the complete shipment lifecycle and technician inspection records across the railway supply chain.

  • Shipment tracking (CREATED → IN TRANSIT → DELIVERED)
  • Real-time tracking events timeline
  • Risk assessment by technicians
  • Automatic GaugeMarket order sync
  • Public tracking number lookup

End-to-End Supply Chain Flow
1
Component Registered — GaugeMarket Vendor registers component; QR identity created; push_to_udm() fires
2
UDM Record Created — This Portal Digital record synced; lifecycle = REGISTERED; dates tracked
3
Listed & Ordered — GaugeMarket Marketplace Customer places order; payment confirmed; lifecycle = PURCHASED
4
Shipment Created — TMS Vendor dispatches; TMS receives shipment notification; tracking number assigned
5
In Transit — TMS Tracking Events PICKED_UP → IN_TRANSIT → ARRIVED_AT_HUB → OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
6
Delivered → Full Lifecycle Updated TMS → GaugeMarket callback → UDM lifecycle = DELIVERED → Railway Division
